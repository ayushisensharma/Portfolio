ECONOMETRICS · R

The Fiscal Multiplier in India: Empirical Evidence from Post-COVID Stimulus

Examines the effectiveness of fiscal policy in India by estimating the fiscal multiplier before and after COVID-19. The analysis uses VAR models, Granger causality and impulse response functions to study the relationship between government expenditure and economic output.

Gender Gaps in Labour Force Participation: A Comparative Analysis of South Asia, 1990–2025

Analyzes male and female labour force participation in India and selected South Asian countries using World Bank data. The project examines long-term trends, gender gaps, female-to-male participation ratios and changes in participation across different periods.
